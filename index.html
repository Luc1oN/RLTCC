Why Rushbrooke

A club worth being associated with

Heritage

Tennis has been played on this site since 1870. We are the oldest tennis club in Ireland and the oldest surviving croquet club in the country. Davis Cup and Federation Cup ties have been played here.

Community

Around a thousand members, drawn from across East Cork. They are here two, three, four times a week, for years at a time. The clubhouse is busy year-round — not just on match days.

Youth

One of the largest junior programmes in Munster. Our three Opens bring roughly 1,200 additional players and their families onto the grounds each year, including families travelling nationwide for the Junior Open.

Where your name goes

This is the part most clubs can't show you.
We can.

Board ink

Type above — the board updates as you go.

Boards are made by the club to a single template and fitted by us: a dark green field matching our scoreboards, your name reversed out in one colour, mounted at the net post. Position and colour follow the ITF advertising rules that apply to our sanctioned tournaments. Preview only — no artwork is stored or sent anywhere.

Five places your name can go. One of them — the court board — you can try above. The other four are shown as concept mockups while we photograph the real thing through the autumn tournaments and the 2027 season. No business appears on any of them: the places are shown as they will be before they are sold.

1 · The Friends of Rushbrooke wall

Mounted on the clubhouse gable at the main entrance, facing the car park and the road. Every member, visitor and tournament player passes it. Fifteen places — every annual partner, Premier Club Partner through Friends of Rushbrooke, gets one — one standard plaque size, one design template. No business supplies its own artwork, and the club's identity stays dominant.

2 · Clubhouse noticeboards

The boards outside the clubhouse carry the draws, the order of play and club notices. During tournament weeks they are read constantly. Partner branding sits alongside.

3 · Interior banners

The main hallway is the first thing anyone sees coming through the door. Reserved for our Premier Partner and the Tournament Partner.

4 · Your name on the court

Partners who take a named court get a board on the net at the post, the same size and style as our scoreboards. It is in front of every player on that court, every time they play, and it is in the background of every photograph taken down the court.

5 · Named facilities

Where a business contributes to something physical at the club — seating, shelter, equipment, groundworks — we mark it with a discreet permanent plaque. This is our quietest form of recognition and, for the right business, the most durable.

Our branding rules, in plain terms

One template. One size. Club identity always dominant. Court boards sit at the net post, in club colours, within the ITF advertising rules — never across the centre of the net, never on the playing surface, never on umpire chairs. All temporary tournament signage comes down within 48 hours. We are not going to cover the place in advertising, and you would not want us to.

Annual partnerships

Four ways in, for the season

Annual partnerships run for the calendar season. Each one carries exclusivity in your trading category — one estate agent, one physio, one broker, one dealership. Founding rates are available in 2027 only. Commit for three years and we hold your founding rate for the full term.

All prices are stated exclusive of VAT.

Tournament partnerships

A good way to test the water

Separate to the annual partnerships, and a lighter commitment. All tournament branding is for the duration of the event only and creates no entitlement in later seasons. Courts already named under an annual partnership are not available.

All prices are stated exclusive of VAT.

Where the money goes

Ring-fenced, and reported

Every euro of partnership income is ring-fenced. It does not disappear into general funds. The split is agreed by the Committee and reported to members every year.

At the AGM and once a season in our newsletter we publish exactly what our partners contributed and exactly what it paid for, and we name and thank every one of them.

A note on what we won't do

We will never pass member contact details to a partner — not names, not emails, not phone numbers. If there is something worth telling our members about, it goes out from the club, clearly marked, with an opt-out. Gambling and betting partnerships are closed to us. Alcohol branding has no place at our junior events.
